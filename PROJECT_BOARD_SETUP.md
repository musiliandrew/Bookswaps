## 🎯 Pro Tips for Project Management

### Daily Workflow
1. **Move cards** from Sprint Planning → In Progress when starting work
2. **Update progress** in card comments
3. **Move to Testing** when feature is complete
4. **Move to Done** when tested and approved
5. **Move to Released** when deployed to production

### Weekly Reviews
1. **Review Sprint Planning** - What's coming next?
2. **Check In Progress** - Any blockers or delays?
3. **Celebrate Done items** - What did we accomplish?
4. **Plan next sprint** - Move items from Backlog to Sprint Planning

### Monthly Planning
1. **Review Backlog** - Prioritize based on user feedback
2. **Update timelines** - Adjust Q2/Q3/Q4 targets
3. **Add new features** - Based on market research
4. **Remove outdated items** - Keep backlog clean and relevant
